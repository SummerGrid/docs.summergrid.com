Getting started with your VPS

Just got your brand new VPS? This guide will help you get started quickly and easily.

1. Accessing your VPS

To access your VPS, you'll need to use SSH (Secure Shell).

  1. First open a terminal such as Command Prompt (Windows), Terminal (macOS), or any terminal emulator (Linux).
  2. Check your VPS creation email for the IP address, username, and password of your VPS.
  3. Inside the terminal enter the command "ssh username@ip_address". (Replace "username" and "ip_address" with the actual values from your email). Then press Enter to run it.
  4. Now you will be prompted to enter the VPS password. Enter the password which you have been provided in the email.
    Note: When typing the password, you won't see any characters appear on the screen. This is a security feature. Just type the password and press Enter.
  5. Now you should be inside your VPS terminal session. Here you can run commands and use your VPS.

2. First steps

Now that you are in your VPS here is some essential first steps you should take.

  1. Important: Secure your VPS immediately using our guide "How to secure your VPS account".
  2. Important: When you first get your VPS if you have ordered additional storage you should expand your root drive to use the full storage capacity. Follow our guide "How to expand your root drive" to do this.
  3. You should then change your VPS's hostname. This is important for easy identification of your VPS and is important to be correct. Follow our guide "How to change the hostname of your VPS" to do this.
