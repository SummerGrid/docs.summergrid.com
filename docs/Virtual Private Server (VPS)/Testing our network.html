Testing out network

Need to check if our network will be suitable for your needs? Use our testing tools!

  • Speed Test: Use our (HTTPS) Speed Test / (HTTP) Speed Test to measure your connection speed to our servers. This will give you an idea of the download and upload speeds you can expect when using our services.
  • Ping / traceroute: Use our Ping / Traceroute Tool to check the latency and route taken by packets to reach our servers. This can help identify any potential network issues or bottlenecks.
Need any help? Just create a support ticket in your hosting dashboard and our friendly team will help you out!
